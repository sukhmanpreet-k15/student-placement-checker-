# student-placement-checker-
This will help student to find whether they can get placed in future or not , by conducting  some tests to check their capability.
<br>
Test will be in 4 parts :<br>
Part 1: There will be mcq related to concepts of oops , dsa , operating system , etc<br>
Part 2: Here coding skills will be tested.<br>
Part 3: In this part usrer will submit their project and analysis will be done to give score.<br>
Part 4: Here user will be asked about their cgpa , number of internships , number of projects were made , and number of backlogs .<br>
